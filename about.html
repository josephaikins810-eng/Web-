About SRC Sports Academy

Our academy was founded to bring professional-level sports training and a strong campus community to every student.

Our Mission

We empower students through sport, teaching discipline, confidence, and teamwork while building performance excellence.

Our History

SRC Sports Academy began as a university athletics initiative and has grown into a full training center supporting both recreational and competitive programs.

What We Value

Respect, dedication, safety, and growth are at the heart of every session. We offer supportive coaching with high performance standards.
